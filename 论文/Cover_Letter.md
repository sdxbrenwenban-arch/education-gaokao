# Cover Letter

Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit the manuscript titled "Descriptive Evidence on the Gender of China's Gaokao Top Scorers: A New Database of 2,598 Records across 30 Provinces (2000–2026)" for consideration at your journal.

This paper introduces the first systematic province-level database of China's Gaokao (national college entrance exam) top scorers. The database covers 30 provinces, 2,598 records from 1956 to 2026, with variables on gender, scores, high-school affiliation, college destinations, tie/bonus-point indicators, and institutional notes on scoring-system reforms. It is accompanied by a province-year institutional panel and a data-driven gender-imputation procedure (back-test accuracy: 91.4% female / 88.7% male among classifiable names).

Using this database, we document three sets of stylized facts: (1) A persistent gender gap across tracks—76.6% of humanities-track top scorers are female versus 39.3% in the science track, robust to bounds analysis for unconfirmed observations; (2) Enormous provincial heterogeneity—the science-track female share ranges from 4.3% in Jiangxi to 56% in Jiangsu; (3) Institutional coupling—446 tied observations are concentrated in standard-score provinces with zero cross-gender ties, and the nationwide ban on top-scorer publicity aligns closely with provincial anonymization timelines (Qinghai 2018 earliest, covering 9 provinces by 2026).

We position this as a data-and-stylized-facts contribution. The database, code, and institutional panel are publicly available on GitHub. We believe the paper will interest readers concerned with gender disparities in high-stakes competition, the economics of merit visibility, and the long-term trajectories of elite exam performance in the world's largest education system.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
The Authors
